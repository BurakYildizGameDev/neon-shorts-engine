5. RENDERING PIPELINE & HARDWARE ACCELERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Neon Shorts Engine uses a zero-dependency, dual-pass WebCodecs architecture:

- **Pass 1 (Pre-Simulation & DSP Synthesis):**
  Calculates all 1,680 frames (28.0 seconds @ 60 FPS) in pure headless JavaScript.
  Events (bounces, eliminations, clangs) record precise millisecond timestamps 
  into the DSP Sound Synthesizer (`SoundSynth.js`). The audio is rendered into 
  an interleaved 44.1 kHz stereo Float32 buffer, passed through a soft Tanh 
  limiter to prevent digital clipping, and compressed via native AAC encoding.

- **Pass 2 (OffscreenCanvas & Video Encoding):**
  The simulation executes visually on an isolated `OffscreenCanvas` at 1080x1920.
  Each rendered frame is captured as a `VideoFrame` and submitted directly to 
  the GPU-accelerated H.264 video encoder (`VideoEncoder` High Profile @ Level 5.2).

- **Muxing & Output:**
  `mp4-muxer` wraps the elementary H.264 video and AAC audio streams into a 
  standard, fully seekable `.mp4` file. Average render time: 12–18 seconds!

8. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why is rendering slow or dropping frames?
A: Ensure hardware acceleration is enabled in Chrome Settings -> System -> 
   "Use graphics acceleration when available".

Q: Where are the audio files stored?
A: No external audio files exist! All sounds are procedurally generated in real 
   time using DSP math. All audio is 100% royalty-free and copyright-safe.

Q: How can I change the video duration?
A: Every game mode class defines `this.duration = 28.0;`. You can adjust this 
   property in `src/modes/<ModeName>.js` if shorter or longer clips are desired.
